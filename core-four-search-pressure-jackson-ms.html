What every number means: average Google searches per month for that exact keyword in that market. Source: Google Keyword Planner.

Brand name — searches for the make itself (example: people typing “bmw” or “audi”).

Top 5 models — searches for the five highest model keywords we measured for that make (example: “bmw x5”, “lexus gx 550”).

Combined pressure — brand name searches + those top five model searches added together. Demand pressure across makes — not a headcount of unique shoppers (overlap possible).

Markets: Jackson, MS DMA (PMA for BMW of Jackson) · Memphis DMA (major adjacent outside) · Mississippi state · plus Hattiesburg-Laurel and Biloxi-Gulfport for other MS DMAs.
