The point

Audi is playing in a smaller search market than BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Lexus.

In Baton Rouge, brand-root demand for bmw / mercedes benz / lexus sits in the 2,900 band. audi sits lower at 1,600 — about 55% of each of the other three on the head term. Same story outside the market: NOLA puts Audi below the other three again.

What this is (and isn’t)

Keep the framing tight when you show it
Is Google’s estimate of how often people search each brand name in each DMA
Shows Audi has meaningfully less brand-root demand than the other core-four makes here and next door
Is not Website traffic, ad performance, or “we’re losing” — it’s market size on the head term
Not saying Don’t invest in Audi. Saying: expect a smaller pond than BMW / MB / Lexus on brand search
