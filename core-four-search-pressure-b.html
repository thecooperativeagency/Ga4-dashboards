Core four search pressure

How often people on Google search each core-four brand name and that brand’s top model keywords in the Baton Rouge market and the New Orleans market next door.
Google Keyword Planner · Google Search · English · pull date 2026-07-30

What every number means: average Google searches per month for that exact keyword in that market. Source: Google Keyword Planner.

Brand name — searches for the make itself (example: people typing “bmw” or “audi”).

Top 5 models — searches for the five highest model keywords we measured for that make (example: “bmw x5”, “audi q5”).

Combined pressure — brand name searches + those top five model searches added together. A simple way to compare demand across makes. Same person can search more than one keyword in a month, so this is demand pressure — not a headcount of unique shoppers.
