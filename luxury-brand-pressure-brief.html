Decision verdict

  1. In the Baton Rouge DMA, Audi brand-root demand sits behind BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Lexus (1,600 vs 2,900 each on exact brand keywords). ABR is not swimming in the same brand-search pool as BHBMW.
  2. Store traffic vs brand pressure: last 30 days GA4 sessions — BHBMW 28,308 · ABR 18,489. Against BTR brand-root demand, both stores’ search-channel capture indexes are similar (~4× organic+paid search sessions per brand-root monthly search). ABR is not “failing BMW’s game”; it has a smaller brand-demand base.
  3. ABR paid is leaving brand coverage on the table: Brand campaign search impression share ~58%, with ~34% lost to budget and only ~8% to rank. Brand spend is only ~$150 / 30 days at ~$0.39 CPC with strong conversions. Draw the first line here: fund brand until budget-lost IS ≈ 0.
  4. Make-model pressure for stocking + bids: BTR DMA — BMW X5 / X6 lead, then X3/X7. Audi — Q5 leads, then Q8 / Q7 / Q3. Outside market (New Orleans DMA) is larger on every brand; use it for conquest reach, not as the primary stocking mirror for BTR metal.
  5. Spend line order: (1) finish ABR brand budget-loss → (2) model/local (Q5, Q7, “audi baton rouge”) → (3) conquest only after 1–2 hold CPA. Stop when losses flip from budget to rank and incremental CPA breaks target.

Where to draw the line

  1. Brand: Raise budget until search budget lost IS ≈ 0%. Keep funding while CPA/lead quality holds. Only then treat remaining gap as rank/competitors.
  2. Model / local: Q5, Q7, “audi baton rouge”, service — fund when IS loss is still budget-driven; if loss is mostly rank (as Q5/Q7 are now), fix bids/quality/creative before dumping budget.
  3. Conquest: Hard-cap by CPA vs close rate. Do not scale while brand still shows double-digit budget-lost IS.
  4. Stop: Next dollar is almost all rank war and conversions flat — that is the line.

Rough brand upside math (directional): Brand is at 58% IS with strong conv. Clearing the ~34% budget-loss band is the cheapest incremental traffic on the account — not matching BMW’s 28k sessions.

5 · Tie-in · inventory buying (ABR)

Market search is a demand prior — still subject to turn, front-end gross, and aged metal on the ground
  • Q5 first among Audi crossovers in BTR search pressure; keep depth aligned to turn.
  • Q8 / Q7 / Q3 secondary band — don’t starve if aged units already sit.
  • Sedans (A4/A6) lower search pressure than crossovers — stock tighter unless gross/turn justify.
  • Outside DMA (NOLA) shows larger absolute model demand — use for paid geo radius tests, not as a mandate to mirror NOLA mix on the BTR lot.
  • Core-four reality: more shoppers start on BMW / MB / Lexus than Audi here — offers and conquest creative should steal on model/value, not on “equal brand heat.”
