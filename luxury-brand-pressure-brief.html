Decision verdict

  1. Planner numbers are real Google estimates, not exact counts. Volumes are bucketed (rungs like 1,600 / 2,900 / 8,100 / 18,100). Use them for ranking and tiers — not “exactly 18,100 people.”
  2. Louisiana brand-root: BMW 18,100 · Tesla 14,800 · MB/Lexus 12,100 · Cadillac 9,900 · Audi/Porsche/Genesis 8,100. Audi sits clearly below the BMW/MB/Lexus band on the head term.
  3. Root + top 5 models (LA stack): BMW 32,300 · Lexus 30,000 · Tesla 26,100 · Cadillac 19,210 · Acura 19,500 · Porsche 17,580 · Genesis 16,980 · MB 16,650 · Audi 15,080 · Infiniti 11,300. Stacks can overlap keywords — not unique searchers.
  4. BTR PMA models: BMW led by X5/X6; Audi by Q5 then Q8/Q7/Q3; Lexus model demand is heavy (GX 550 / RX 350). Stock and bids should follow model pressure, not brand-root alone.
  5. Store capture: BHBMW 28,308 vs ABR 18,489 sessions (30d). Search capture index vs brand-root is essentially tied (~4.1× vs ~4.2×). ABR’s lower traffic tracks a smaller demand base more than poor capture.
  6. ABR spend line: Brand paid IS ~58% with ~34% lost to budget. Fund brand until budget-lost IS ≈ 0, then model/local, conquest last.

1 · How accurate is Planner?

Straight answer before the tables
QuestionAnswer
Is 18,100 / 8,100 a real estimate?Yes — Google’s own Keyword Planner average monthly searches for that exact keyword in Louisiana.
Is it exact?No — volumes snap to buckets (rungs). Not 18,147 people.
What is it good for?Ranking brands/models, tiering demand, spend/stock direction.
What is it bad for?Counting unique buyers, precise forecasts, or treating identical bucket values as true equality.
Why do BMW / MB / Lexus all show 2,900 in BTR?Same bucket band in a small DMA — not proof of identical demand. LA statewide separates them (18.1k / 12.1k / 12.1k).

Where to draw the line

  1. Brand first — raise budget until budget-lost IS ≈ 0%, while CPA holds.
  2. Model / local next — Q5, Q7, audi baton rouge, service. If loss is mostly rank (Q5/Q7 now), fix bids/quality before pouring budget.
  3. Conquest last — hard-cap by CPA vs close rate; don’t scale while brand is budget-capped.
  4. Stop when the next dollar is almost all rank war and conversions are flat.

6 · Inventory buying tie-in (ABR)

  • Q8 / Q7 / Q3 Secondary crossover band.
  • Sedans A5 leads sedans; A4/A6 lower — stock tighter unless gross justifies.
  • Core four More shoppers start on BMW / MB / Lexus than Audi — conquest on model/value, not equal brand heat.
  • NOLA Larger absolute demand — paid radius yes; don’t mirror NOLA mix on the BTR lot 1:1.
